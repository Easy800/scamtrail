# 删除与保留方案

ST-DEV-001 §24.5.

- Users may request deletion of private material that has not entered a necessary dispute or a completed disclosure.
- Delete object-store originals, derivatives, caches, and search indexes.
- Keep a minimal **Tombstone** that records the fact of deletion, not the payload.
- Re-evaluate public Claims if they lose their last independent Evidence.
- Completed disclosures cannot be recalled from the recipient; say so before submit.
- Retention days are configuration per jurisdiction, not hardcoded.
- “Immutable” means no in-place overwrite during retention. It does not refuse lawful deletion.
