# 申诉与改判政策

A dispute targets one or more **Claims**, never “the whole case” as a single switch (ST-DEV-001 §17).

- Retracting “the photographed person controls this account” does not retract “a scam occurred” or “the account was used to solicit payment.”
- While under review, public pages show: `Previously verified — under review / 该主张此前已核验，目前因新反证正在复核。`
- Outcomes: `UPHELD`, `MODIFIED`, `PARTIALLY_RETRACTED`, `RETRACTED`, `IDENTITY_MISATTRIBUTION`, `DUPLICATE_OR_COORDINATED_SUBMISSION`.
- After a Decision: update every citing Case, Campaign stats, Indicator, search index, public changelog, eligible submitters, and any undisclosed Collective Case package.

EXIF-earlier is not an automatic win. Independently verifiable early publication plus original files is stronger than metadata alone.
